Hiraeth: The Adventures of Bevel and Logan

Trickster Wish Lords, Colossal Storm Creatures, Blackhearted Tyrants, Assassin Dogs... and a Schoolyard Bully.
‍
The stuff of childhood nightmares? This is Hiraeth!

Running from a funeral, a grieving boy and a monster journey into a dark fantasy world on a rescue mission. They might have to save a kingdom while they’re at it.
01

untimely

On the day of his grandfather’s funeral, Logan struggles to find his place. Nothing feels right—the adults are laughing, his questions go unanswered, and he's stuck with cousins he barely knows. But when he stumbles into his grandfather’s old workshop, what he finds in the dark may change everything he thought he knew about saying goodbye.

02

Heartfelt

Logan plunges into the perilous Passage Realm, a labyrinth of shifting dangers and hidden traps. With a monster named Bevel at his side, he pushes forward—uneasy, yet gripping the fragile, impossible hope that his grandfather might still be alive.

how our shop works

We do things a little differently at Box Fort. Our shop over at Big Cartel only opens a few times a year, and just for a few weeks at a time. Not exactly business-school approved—but it lets us focus most of the year on making the best comics we can. When the shop is open, we pour all our energy into packing orders and getting books into your hands fast. The rest of the year, we’re heads-down making the next issue. If you can't wait, you can catch us at conventions, pop-ups, and comic shop events where we’ll always have books on hand to sell. Check back here to see if the shop is open—or, better yet, sign up for the newsletter below so you never miss a drop.
