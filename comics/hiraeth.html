Hiraeth: The Adventures of Bevel and Logan

For Logan, being seven isn’t easy. He’s full of big feelings and even bigger questions. But the adults in his life don’t take him seriously.

On the day of his grandfather’s funeral, Logan runs away and stumbles into Hiraeth, a dark fantasy world of strange magic, dangerous trials, and grave truths. With a monster named Bevel at his side, he sets out to rescue the one person he can’t bear to lose--and he might have to save a kingdom along the way.

Trickster Wish Lords, Colossal Storm Creatures, Blackhearted Tyrants, Assassin Dogs… and a Schoolyard Bully.

Hiraeth is a fantasy adventure about friendship, courage, and the wild, messy work of grief.

RATING: KIDS OF ALL AGES &
FOR ANYONE WHO'S LOST SOMEONE
01

untimely

On the day of his grandfather’s funeral, Logan struggles to find his place. Nothing feels right—the adults are laughing, his questions go unanswered, and he's stuck with cousins he barely knows. But when he stumbles into his grandfather’s old workshop, what he finds in the dark may change everything he thought he knew about saying goodbye.

02

Heartfelt

Logan plunges into the perilous Passage Realm, a labyrinth of shifting dangers and hidden traps. With a monster named Bevel at his side, he pushes forward—uneasy, yet gripping the fragile, impossible hope that his grandfather might still be alive.

03

wayward

Lost on a vast ocean, Logan and Bevel climb aboard a battered ship crewed by a lone, possibly unhinged deckhand. What happened to the captain and crew? Can they escape a maelstrom that seems almost alive? This will be their fiercest battle yet. To survive, they’ll have to weather it together. Set sail for adventure in Hiraeth Chapter Three!
