scott prather art portfolio

Originally from Fort Worth, Texas, Scott earned his BFA in Illustration from Ringling College of Art and Design in Florida. Since returning home, he’s spent the last decade making his mark as a tattoo artist—earning recognition, accolades, and a host of loyal fans along the way. While proud of his tattoo career, Scott has always carried a deeper passion for creating images that tell stories. Now, he’s bringing that dream to life through comics. Scott is supported by his wife, Becky, their daughter, Kailey, and their three dogs: Scratch, Tully, and Odie Lou.
